3. What is regression testing in a release context? [Regression testing is the process of rerunning existing tests before release to confirm that previously working functionality still works correctly after recent changes, fixes or final edits.]

4. What is a known limitation? [A known limitation is a recognised constraint or shortcoming of the current system. It differs from an unintended defect because the limitation is understood and documented and may be accepted as outside the current release scope.]

5. What is technical debt? [Technical debt refers to shortcuts or design decisions that make development easier or faster in the short term but may require additional work later. Examples can include duplicated code, limited scalability, weak structure or improvements that have been deliberately deferred.]

6. What are release notes? [Release notes explain what has changed in a particular version of software. They commonly describe new features, bug fixes, important changes, known limitations and other information relevant to users or developers.]

7. What is software maintenance? [Software maintenance is the ongoing work performed after software has been developed or released, including correcting defects, improving functionality, adapting the software to new requirements and keeping it reliable and maintainable.]

[The individual demonstration or viva allows each team member to demonstrate their own understanding of the system, explain their contribution to the project and show that they can discuss the design, implementation and testing decisions made by the team.]
